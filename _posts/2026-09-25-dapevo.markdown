We introduce DAPEVO, a learned visual odometry system that adaptively combines image and event correspondences.
Separate encoders track shared patches in each modality, while a learned gate weights their matching evidence before
recurrent refinement and bundle adjustment. The system continues tracking with event-only observations when RGB frames
are sparse or unavailable. On TartanEvent, DAPEVO maintains an absolute trajectory error below 1 m at 3 Hz RGB input,
while DPVO and RAMP-VO exceed 9 m, and achieves 0.60 m under degraded RGB input.
